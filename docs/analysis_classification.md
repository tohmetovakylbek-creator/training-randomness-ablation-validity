# Analysis classification

## Preregistered

- The ECL replication eligibility rule, sampled clients, estimand, and success criteria, as recorded before the first replication training run.

## Pre-specified within an analysis protocol

- The five-seed generality runs for PatchTST and BiLSTM.
- The OpenML-CC18 task selection, five seeds, primary balanced-error metric, secondary cross-entropy metric, and registered success criterion, fixed before inspection of the final CC18 outcomes.

## Exploratory and post-hoc sensitivity analyses

- Decomposition-branch and gated-fusion diagnostics.
- OpenML-CC18 feature-identity benchmark as an extension beyond the original energy-domain design.
- CC18 prediction-flip diagnostic.
- CC18 restrictions to datasets with at least 150 or 250 test observations.
- Alternative block lengths and clustered or three-level meta-analytic sensitivity analyses where reported.

The manuscript must preserve this distinction and must not describe a pre-specified analysis as preregistered unless it has a dated external registration record.
